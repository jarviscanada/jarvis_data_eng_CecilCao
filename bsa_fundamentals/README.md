# Introduction
As a business analyst, the goal of this project was to meet with an associate from the Bank of Jarvis in order to draft a software requirements document that can capture the trading app they wished to deploy with the rest of their online service products. The goal for them was to have a way for their customers to make market orders online, rather than in person at their local service branch. In addition, their competition has also transitioned towards online investing services, and they would like to keep competitive with this addition to their product suite.
After an elicitation meeting with the associate, we were able to confirm the Bank of Jarvis wished to deploy a web and mobile interface within 8 months. They also wished to start small, and only allow customers to access the app after signing up at a service branch. The app would also be only allowed to perform market orders. Their target demographic would be their over 150,000 customers, primarily focused on their small to medium business customers.
After the meeting, a software requirements document was drafted. It outlines the overall user flow, and focuses on providing a simplified trading experience in order to accommodate new investors, and those unfamiliar to IoT technology. Each major feature, such as user login and placing market orders, and outlined functional requirements, and an overarching decision flow that users should be expected to perform when using the feature.

# Improvements
1. The document does not line a specific framework, but rather suggestions of popular frameworks for development. Meetings should be held with the development team to create a list of frameworks that would work best with the team.
2. The document does not provide a way to break down the tasks into an easy timeline, aside from prioritizing user features. Further breaking down the development timeline would make it easier to estimate completion date of the application.
